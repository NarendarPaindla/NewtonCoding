Newton's Institute of Engineering (NIE), located in Macherla, Palnadu District, Andhra Pradesh, is a premier institution committed to providing quality technical education and fostering innovation among students. Established in 2001, the institute is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and affiliated with Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Kakinada

The institute offers a wide range of undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programs in Engineering, Management, and Computer Applications. With modern infrastructure, well-equipped laboratories, experienced faculty members, and industry-oriented training programs, the college focuses on developing both technical expertise and professional skills among students.

Spread across a spacious and eco-friendly campus, Newton's Institute of Engineering provides an excellent learning environment that encourages academic excellence, research, innovation, and extracurricular participation. The institution emphasizes practical learning through workshops, seminars, internships, and industry collaborations to prepare students for successful careers in the rapidly evolving technological world.

The college has a dedicated Training and Placement Cell that works closely with leading companies to provide placement opportunities, career guidance, and skill development programs. Through its commitment to quality education and holistic student development, Newton's Institute of Engineering continues to shape competent professionals and responsible citizens who contribute positively to society.
